1. Who we are

VedicAstro ("we", "us", "our") provides an AI-assisted Vedic astrology platform, including birth chart (Kundli) generation, daily/weekly/monthly/yearly horoscopes, Panchang, compatibility matching, and an AI chat assistant. This policy explains what information we collect through vedicastro.com (or wherever this site is hosted) and how we use it.

2. Information we collect

Account information. When you create an account: your name, email address, and a securely hashed password (we never store your password in plain text).

Birth details. When you generate a Kundli, check compatibility, or use related features: date of birth, time of birth, and birth place (for you and, for compatibility checks, a second person). This is used to calculate real astronomical/astrological data — planetary positions, houses, and related results — for the chart(s) you request.

AI conversation history. Questions you ask our AI Assistant and its responses, which may reference your birth chart to give personalized answers.

Usage data. Basic technical information such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited, collected automatically for security and to keep the service running reliably.

3. How we use your information

  • To generate the charts, horoscopes, and compatibility reports you request
  • To ground the AI Assistant's answers in your actual birth chart
  • To maintain your account and let you revisit saved charts
  • To send you account-related emails (such as password reset links)
  • To maintain the security and reliability of the service

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Third-party services we use

Some of your data is processed by third-party services we rely on to run VedicAstro:

  • xAI (Grok) — birth chart details and your questions are sent to xAI's API to generate AI Assistant responses and AI-written horoscope/Panchang text. See xAI's own privacy policy for how they handle this data.
  • Resend — used to send account-related emails (such as password reset links). Your email address is shared with Resend for this purpose only.
  • Database and hosting providers (e.g. Supabase, Vercel) — store and serve the application and its data.

5. Data storage and security

Passwords are hashed, never stored in plain text. Password reset links are single-use, expire after 1 hour, and are stored as a hash rather than the raw link. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Your choices and rights

  • You can view and revisit your saved Kundlis while signed in via "My Kundlis"
  • You can request that we delete your account and associated data by contacting us at [your-contact-email@example.com]
  • You can stop using the AI Assistant or compatibility features at any time — using them is optional

7. Children's privacy

VedicAstro is intended for users aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
